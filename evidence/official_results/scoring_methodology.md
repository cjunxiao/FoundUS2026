
# Organizer Scoring Methods

## Leaderboard A / Method 1

For every task and metric, scores are Min-Max normalized across the 31 teams.
Normalized values are averaged across the nine tasks to form MRE and MAE
dimension scores, then combined with equal weights. Prize money is based on
this original leaderboard.

## Leaderboard B / Method 2

Raw MRE and MAE values are first averaged across the nine tasks. The two
aggregate values are then separately Min-Max normalized across the 31 teams and
combined with equal weights.

Team cjunxiao appears on both leaderboards, so the organizer requires one
materials package named from the Leaderboard A rank: `12_cjunxiao_MWM95.zip`.
All ranks remain preliminary pending final eligibility verification.
